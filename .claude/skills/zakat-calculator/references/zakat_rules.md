# Zakat Rules Reference

## Nisab Thresholds

| Standard | Weight                        | Approx. USD (2024) |
| -------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------ |
| Gold     | 87.48 grams (≈ 3 troy oz)     | ~$6,500–$7,500     |
| Silver   | 612.36 grams (≈ 21.5 troy oz) | ~$450–$600         |

- Most contemporary scholars recommend **silver nisab** because the lower threshold means more people qualify to pay, benefiting more recipients.
- The nisab must be met for **one full lunar year (hawl)** — 354 days.

## Zakat Rate

- **2.5%** (1/40th) on net zakatable wealth for most categories.
- If using a **solar year**, the adjusted rate is **2.5775%**.

## Zakatable Wealth Categories

### 1. Cash & Bank Balances

- All cash, savings, checking, and fixed deposits.
- Includes money in digital wallets (PayPal, Venmo, etc.).

### 2. Gold & Silver

- All gold and silver owned, including jewelry, coins, and bullion.
- **Scholarly difference on jewelry**:
  - **Hanafi**: All gold/silver jewelry is zakatable regardless of use.
  - **Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali**: Personal-use jewelry (worn regularly) is exempt.
- Use the weight in grams × current market price per gram.

### 3. Stocks & Investments

- **Long-term holdings**: Zakat on the full market value.
- **Active trading**: Zakat on full market value (treated as trade goods).
- Mutual funds, ETFs, REITs — use current market value.
- **Retirement accounts (401k, IRA)**: Scholars differ. Conservative view includes them if accessible.

### 4. Cryptocurrency

- Treated like cash or trade goods — zakat on full market value.
- Use the market value at the time of zakat calculation (or average over hawl period).
- Staked crypto and DeFi positions are included.

### 5. Business Inventory

- Trade goods held for resale at current market value.
- Equipment used in business (not for sale) is **not** zakatable.
- Raw materials intended for production and sale **are** zakatable.

### 6. Receivables

- Money owed to you that you expect to receive — include at full value.
- Bad debts (unlikely to be repaid) — some scholars exclude these.

### 7. Rental Property

- The **property itself** is not zakatable.
- **Accumulated rental income** (cash saved from rent) is zakatable.

## Deductions (Liabilities)

- Debts due within the current year.
- Bills and expenses payable immediately.
- Business liabilities related to zakatable trade goods.
- **Long-term debts**: Only the portion due within 12 months is deducted (majority view).

## Zakat Calculation Formula

```
Gross Zakatable Wealth = Cash + Gold + Silver + Stocks + Crypto + Inventory + Receivables
Net Zakatable Wealth   = Gross Zakatable Wealth − Deductions
Zakat Due              = Net Zakatable Wealth × 0.025   (if ≥ nisab)
Zakat Due              = 0                               (if < nisab)
```

## Eight Categories of Zakat Recipients (Asnaf)

1. **Al-Fuqara** — The poor
2. **Al-Masakin** — The needy
3. **Al-Amilin** — Zakat administrators
4. **Al-Mu'allafah** — Those whose hearts are to be reconciled
5. **Ar-Riqab** — Freeing captives
6. **Al-Gharimin** — Debtors
7. **Fi Sabilillah** — In the cause of Allah
8. **Ibn As-Sabil** — Wayfarers / travelers in need

## Edge Cases

- **Joint accounts**: Each person calculates based on their share.
- **Minors' wealth**: Most scholars say a guardian should pay zakat on a minor's wealth.
- **Debt-funded assets**: If you used a loan to buy stocks, deduct outstanding loan amount.
- **Zakatable vs. non-zakatable mixing**: If unclear, err on the side of inclusion.
