# Sharia Screening Criteria Reference

## AAOIFI Standard No. 21 — Investment in Shares

The Accounting and Auditing Organization for Islamic Financial Institutions (AAOIFI) provides the most widely adopted framework for screening equity investments for Sharia compliance.

---

## Phase 1: Qualitative Screening (Business Activity)

### Fully Prohibited Sectors

Companies whose **core business** involves any of the following are automatically non-compliant:

| Sector                     | Description                                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Alcohol**                | Manufacturing, distributing, or selling alcoholic beverages  |
| **Gambling**               | Casinos, sports betting, lotteries, gambling platforms       |
| **Conventional Finance**   | Interest-based banking, conventional insurance, credit cards |
| **Weapons**                | Manufacturing or trading in weapons and ammunition           |
| **Tobacco**                | Manufacturing or distributing tobacco products               |
| **Adult Entertainment**    | Pornography and related industries                           |
| **Pork**                   | Pork production, processing, or distribution                 |
| **Interest-based Lending** | Payday loans, consumer credit, auto financing with riba      |

### Mixed-Activity Companies

Companies with permissible core businesses but some non-compliant activities may still qualify if they pass the quantitative screens below.

**Examples of mixed companies:**

- Airlines (serve alcohol in-flight)
- Hotels (may have bars/casinos)
- Conglomerates with some financial services divisions
- Tech companies with interest income on cash reserves

## Phase 2: Quantitative Screening (Financial Ratios)

### AAOIFI Thresholds (Primary Standard)

| #   | Ratio             | Formula                                                      | Max Threshold |
| --- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- |
| 1   | **Debt Ratio**    | Interest-bearing debt ÷ Market capitalization                | **< 30%**     |
| 2   | **Cash Ratio**    | (Cash + Interest-bearing securities) ÷ Market capitalization | **< 30%**     |
| 3   | **Impure Income** | Non-permissible income ÷ Total revenue                       | **< 5%**      |

### Ratio Explanations

#### 1. Debt Ratio (< 30%)

- Measures reliance on interest-based (riba) borrowing.
- **Interest-bearing debt**: Long-term debt + short-term borrowings + bonds payable that carry interest.
- **Market capitalization**: Share price × total shares outstanding.
- **Exceeding 30%** indicates the company is significantly financed by riba.

#### 2. Cash Ratio (< 30%)

- Ensures the company is not primarily a cash/interest-holding entity.
- **Cash + interest-bearing securities**: Cash & equivalents + short-term investments + marketable securities earning interest.
- Companies holding excessive cash in interest-bearing accounts are problematic.

#### 3. Impure Income Ratio (< 5%)

- Non-permissible income from secondary/incidental activities.
- Examples: Interest income, penalties charged, income from haram subsidiaries.
- **5% threshold** = tolerable level; must be purified through charitable donation.

---

## Purification

When a compliant stock has impure income between 0% and 5%, the investor must **purify** their earnings:

```
Purification Ratio   = Non-permissible income ÷ Total revenue
Purification Amount  = (Dividends + Capital gains) × Purification Ratio
```

This amount must be **donated to charity** (not counted as sadaqah — it's an obligation).

## Comparison: Screening Methodologies

| Criterion          | AAOIFI        | DJIM                  | S&P Sharia    | MSCI Islamic  |
| ------------------ | ------------- | --------------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| Denominator        | Market cap    | Market cap (avg 24mo) | Market cap    | Total assets  |
| Debt threshold     | 30%           | 33%                   | 33%           | 33.33%        |
| Cash threshold     | 30%           | 33%                   | 33%           | 33.33%        |
| Impure income      | 5%            | 5%                    | 5%            | 5%            |
| Prohibited sectors | Standard list | Standard list         | Standard list | Standard list |

### Key Differences

- **AAOIFI** uses market cap as the denominator for all ratios.
- **DJIM** uses 24-month average market cap.
- **MSCI** uses total assets as the denominator (more conservative for high-cap low-asset companies).
- Most indices agree on the **5% impure income** threshold.
